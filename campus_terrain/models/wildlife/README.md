# Original ambient animal models

Four original stylized low-poly GLBs created in Blender by model_previews/wildlife_v01/build.py. Flat material colours; no textures or painted lighting. Separately named heads, tails, legs and wings support lightweight rigid-part animation in scripts/wildlife.gd. No third-party animal assets are used.

Cat: about 0.9 m tall including its upright tail, with roughly 0.7 m body length. Birds: about 0.45 m tall. Adult ducks: approximately 0.6 m long; ducklings use 65 percent scale. Butterflies: roughly 0.45 m wingspan, exaggerated slightly for visibility from the high camera. These are initial stylized ambient models, not final realistic animal art or fully skinned animation rigs.
