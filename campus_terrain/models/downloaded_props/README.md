# Downloaded prop replacements

Prepared copies of the user's 18 November 2026 downloads. Originals in Downloads were never modified. Source file names, evaluated triangle counts, dimensions and measured centreline surface profiles are recorded in source_info.json.

- Bench: park-bench model, 8,000 triangles, placed at 2.1 m width.
- Bridge: graffiti bridge FBX from the new ZIP, 18,000 triangles; fitted to the 19 ? 6 m campus crossing with its deck aligned to the existing +2 m walking surface.
- Stairs: downloaded staircase GLB, 18,000 triangles; fitted to each 2 m campus rise and repeated five times for each 10 m waterfront flight.
- Swing: floral swing GLB, 24,000 triangles; placed at 2.7 m height in the neighbourhood playground. Decorative/static; swinging and sitting interactions are not included.

Models are welded, simplified and texture-limited to 2048 pixels. Geometry and materials come from the user downloads, not the earlier handcrafted review set. Source textures are retained; their authored appearance is not a new guarantee of unbaked source lighting. Existing collision for stairs and the campus bridge is preserved. Bench and swing dressing do not change the established walking routes.

This user-directed replacement uses the downloaded styles, including their ornamental details. No existing reference/, assets3d/, art_source/ or game3d/ files were overwritten.

Reproduction and preparation records: model_previews/downloaded_props_v01/prepare.py, finalize.py, final_report.json.
